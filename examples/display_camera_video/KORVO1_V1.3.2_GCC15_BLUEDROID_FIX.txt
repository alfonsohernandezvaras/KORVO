KORVO1 V1.3.2 - ESP-IDF 6.1 / GCC 15 BLUEDROID BUILD FIX

Problem observed in V1.3.1:
  components/bt/host/bluedroid/bta/dm/bta_dm_act.c
  -Werror=stringop-truncation from BCM_STRNCPY_S / strncpy.

The V1.3.1 CONFIG_COMPILER_DISABLE_DEFAULT_ERRORS=y setting was active, but
ESP-IDF converted -Werror to -Werror=all and GCC 15 still stopped on this
framework warning.

V1.3.2 adds, after project(display_camera_new):
  idf_build_set_property(COMPILE_OPTIONS
                         "-Wno-error=stringop-truncation" APPEND)

This is a build-only compatibility fix. It does not change:
- KORVO/Gateway IP pairing
- SIP logic
- RTP/audio bridge
- Bluetooth HFP AG logic
- camera/HMI/access logic

Recommended build:
  idf.py fullclean
  idf.py build
