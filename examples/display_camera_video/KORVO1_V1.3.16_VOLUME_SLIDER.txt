KORVO1 V1.3.16 - RIGHT EDGE OUTPUT VOLUME

- Adds a vertical 0..100 % VOL slider on the right edge of the normal camera HMI.
- Slider stays outside the central face/camera guide and is hidden behind the full Bluetooth panel.
- Volume is applied directly to 16-bit PCM sent over HFP/SCO, affecting WAV alarms and SIP receive audio.
- Slider changes are immediate; NVS is written only when the touch is released.
- Default volume is 75 %. Stored separately from the existing Bluetooth config blob so pairing/config data is not reset.
- No change to the working HFP pairing/reconnect supervisor.
- PROJECT_VER = 1.3.16.

Not compiled in the ChatGPT environment. Build with ESP-IDF 6.1 in the user's normal project environment.
